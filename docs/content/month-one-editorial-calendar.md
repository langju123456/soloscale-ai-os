evidence, control, learning, delivery, and quality systems. Six canonical stories per
week are production candidates; remaining days are deliberately reserved for recaps,
feedback, repurposing, behind-the-scenes clips, delayed publication, or rest.

## Repurposing rule

Every canonical story may later become LinkedIn, an X thread, an interview answer, a
portfolio case study, or a carousel. The primary format is an editorial recommendation,
not an instruction to render or publish automatically.
